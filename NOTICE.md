# Notice

`SKILL.md` and the files in `references/` combine and adapt writing guidance from the sources below. The combined skill is not released under a license of its own, because The Humanizer carries none. Material from the MIT-licensed sources is used under their terms, and their notices follow the table.

| Source | Used for | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Avoid AI Writing](https://github.com/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing) v3.38.0 with its catalog changes through 2026-11-03, by Conor Bronsdon | Edit contract, modes, severity tiers, output rules; the pattern catalog, decision cards and profiles in `references/patterns.md` | MIT |
| [The Humanizer](https://github.com/adarsh-nellore/cursor-skills/tree/main/the-humanizer) v2.4, by Crazy Marketer | Universal, LinkedIn, email and Slack markers, originality checks, review scoring (`references/channels.md`) | None found |
| [Use conversational language](https://github.com/eai-org/agent-toolkit/tree/main/skills/use-conversational-language) v1.12, by Engineering in the age of AI | Conversational writing, developer messages, code comments, commit messages (`references/conversational.md`) | MIT |
| [anti-slop](https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop) by Miqdad Badjuber | Copywriting and evidence rules (`references/channels.md`) | MIT |
| [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) by Siqi Chen | Patterns marked `blader/humanizer` in the catalog, including four added in 1.3.0 | MIT |
| [humanizer](https://github.com/brandonwise/humanizer) by Brandon Wise | Vocabulary tier research | MIT |
| [humanizer-skill](https://github.com/Aboudjem/humanizer-skill) by Adam Boudjemaa | Patterns marked `Aboudjem/humanizer-skill` in the catalog | MIT |
| [stop-slop-refined](https://github.com/welttowelt/stop-slop-refined) | Patterns credited to `welttowelt/stop-slop-refined` in the catalog | MIT |

The catalog also credits Simon Willison's [LLM cliché highlighter](https://tools.simonwillison.net/llm-cliche-highlighter) (from simonw/tools, Apache-2.0), a [post by Charity Majors](https://charity.wtf/p/confessions-of-an-unrepentant-slop), and tropes.fyi. Its frequency counts come from Avoid AI Writing's exploratory counts on the [HAP-E corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/browndw/human-ai-parallel-corpus); no corpus text is included.

Written for this skill: the Russian-language rules (`references/russian.md`), the fiction rules (`references/fiction.md`), `translate` mode, and the context-fit check. The guidance on officialese follows Корней Чуковский (*Живой как жизнь*, 1962) and Нора Галь (*Слово живое и мёртвое*).
